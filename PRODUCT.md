# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Leaders and staff at small nonprofits and mission-driven organizations, including visitors who use a keyboard or screen reader. They need to quickly understand how Magnify Impact can support their mission, see practical examples, read the founder's thinking, and start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

The public website explains people-first AI integration through customized consulting, training and organizational development, and automation builds. Success means a visitor understands that Magnify Impact builds on their existing good work and can confidently contact Philip.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, thoughtful, and practical. The site should feel clear, capable, humane, and specific to mission-driven work rather than like a generic AI consultancy.

## Anti-references

Avoid AI stock imagery, robots, glowing brains, decorative gradients, autoplay, scroll effects that hide content, invented metrics, fake testimonials, and generic SaaS card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with mission impact and people, not technology.
2. Build on the visitor's existing good work.
3. Make AI possibilities concrete while keeping human judgment visible.
4. Give visitors a direct, low-friction path to connect.
5. Use a distinct but restrained identity that stays readable and useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible focus, keyboard-operable controls, meaningful form labels and feedback, sufficient contrast, responsive text, and reduced-motion-safe behavior. The founder is visually disabled; descriptions and structure must not depend on color or visual inference.
