Ideas for Your Processes

Explore ways to make everyday work easier—and create more room for your mission.

Volunteer coordination

A possible improvement Set event dates, roles, and an outreach schedule. Reminders go out in advance, and volunteer responses feed into a proposed roster with gaps and conflicts flagged.

Your team decides Review and approve the roster. Then confirmations and calendar invitations go out.

Value gained Less chasing replies and copying information. Clearer coverage, with control over final assignments.

Grant writing

A possible improvement Connect grant-writing platforms with funder and donor research tools, where supported, to identify potential matches for your mission. Bring those opportunities together with your approved program information. AI helps tailor each proposal to the funder's priorities, application requirements, and your organization's actual work.

Your team decides Confirm eligibility and funder fit. Check every claim, budget figure, and commitment, fill any information gaps, and approve the final application in your voice before submission.

Value gained Less manual prospect research and repeated drafting. More relevant opportunities and proposals tailored to each funder, with your team in control.

More ways to giveStock & donor-advised fund gifts

A possible improvement Add clear options for donors to give stock from a brokerage account or recommend a grant from a donor-advised fund (DAF), using a giving platform that fits your organization.

Your team decides Choose the platform after reviewing fees, eligibility, and your existing systems. Establish how staff will track received gifts and follow up with donors.

Value gained More giving options for supporters, clearer instructions, and a more organized gift-tracking process.

Examples to evaluate: FreeWill and Overflow.
