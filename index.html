Common Questions

Honest answers.

Why does AI matter for small non-profits and small businesses?

Because your scarcest resource is time. Your organization's time and value is in your mission-driven work you do, and AI can help speed-up or even automate much of the administrative tasks involved in running an organization such as data entry, scheduling, coordination, grant writing, newsletter/communications, and many other processes. AI gives you and your team time back in the day so you can focus on your mission and the benefit your organization brings that AI cannot.

Is there an AI divide between large and small organizations?

Yes — and it's growing. Large enterprises are adopting AI quickly, while most small non-profits and businesses haven't had the time, budget, or guidance to start, and that gap compounds every year. The good news: today's tools no longer require enterprise budgets, and small organizations can close the distance faster than they think. Small organizations can choose and right-size the tools that fit their organization the best without buying a whole enterprise package. Even a small implementation of one tool can unlock tremendous value for a small organization without much investment.

What does working with Magnify Impact look like?

It starts with an honest conversation about your mission, your organization, and where your team's time actually goes. From there we can provide customized orientations and trainings for your leadership and teams. From our conversations with you and your teams, we will identify the highest-impact, cost-effective, ease of implementation AI and automation options that will bring the most value to your organization. We can then build and deploy customized right-sized tools with additional training if needed, so the tools keep working after we step back.

How much does it cost?

We are here to help and not turn a profit. Like you, we believe in the incredible impact of good people in small mission-driven organizations, and that is the community we are committed to serving. Engagements are right-sized, just like the tools. Simple, high-return automations are small scoped projects; complex or higher-risk work starts with paid discovery and honest advice — including when the best answer is a simpler tool, or a referral to someone better suited to build it. The first conversation is always just a suggested donation of an amount that makes sense to you.

How do I know where my organization stands with AI?

Most organizations sit somewhere on a scale — from "haven't started," to a few individuals experimenting, to organization-wide adoption with training and guardrails. Knowing where you stand determines the right next step, and skipping steps is how tools end up unused. That honest discussion is where every engagement of ours begins.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's not the goal, and it's not what we build toward. The goal is removing the repetitive mechanics that keep good people from maximizing their time. AI is best when it enhances and works in tandem with teams to progress organizational goals. AI will not creatively come up with new projects and ideas that further your mission, but it can give your team time to creatively and strategically develop and implement them. AI adoption succeeds when your team is trained, included, and honestly heard. This fear can be directly addressed by your organization's leadership, but studies have shown that organizations who have used AI to replace staff have wound-up rehiring in the following six months.

What about donor and client data privacy?

We treat it as non-negotiable. We help you set a clear AI use policy, choose tools with appropriate data protections, and train staff on what should — and should never — go into an AI system. Nothing touches sensitive data without your explicit decision. You are in control, not AI.
